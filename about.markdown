I ~~have it all figured out~~ keep getting back up, and so far that has been enough.

## What I do

I'm a software engineer from Mumbai, now based in the US. I like building things that listen, talk, and take work off people's plates, especially voice AI. I keep coming back to healthcare, logistics, consumer products and media, where the gap between what AI can do and what actually gets built is still huge.

It started in Pune in 2021 at eQ Technologic, writing enterprise software for clients who could not afford bugs. Then came a master's at [Northeastern](https://www.northeastern.edu) in Boston, an internship at EmTech Care Labs building tools for caregivers, and a software engineer role at [VoiceERP](https://voiceerp.ai), where I helped teach an AI to dispatch delivery drivers. Today I work at an AI startup in Florida, building AI solutions in the SAP space and the aviation industry.

I build things because I've felt the problem first. I want to be remembered as ~~a software engineer who built something awesome~~ someone who thinks like a product person and builds solutions customers can't live without. I believe an engineer's success should be directly proportional to the customer's success. If the customer isn't winning, the work isn't done.

## Things I've built

More of my code lives on [GitHub](https://github.com/aayushNortheastern1905).

- At VoiceERP, a voice agent that schedules drivers for a last mile logistics team, so dispatchers stopped doing it by hand.
- At EmTech Care Labs, tools for caregivers and counselors: session transcripts, benefits eligibility checks, and Medicaid data that updates itself.
- [VeryDesi](https://verydesi.com), a housing platform for hundreds of South Asian students looking for a safe first home in America.
- [A free AI agent for visa paperwork](https://github.com/aayushNortheastern1905/immigration-ai-agent), because nobody should need a lawyer's patience to fill out a form.
- A bilingual voice agent called Sage that qualifies lending leads, where plain code, not the model, makes every approval decision. [Call it](tel:+17742478006) at (774) 247-8006 and talk to it yourself.
- [An autonomous coding agent](https://github.com/aayushNortheastern1905/sarvam-code-agent) that checks its own patches in a sandbox and cannot spend past its budget.
- A [voice controlled wheelchair model](https://doi.org/10.1109/GCAT52182.2021.9587859), published with IEEE back in college. Voice has been on my mind longer than it's been fashionable.
